ColorCraft — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

ColorCraft turns what a child types, says, or photographs into a black-and-white colouring page. To do that, the words and any photo they choose are sent to an AI service that draws the picture, and the picture comes back.

We do not ask for a name, an email address, or an account. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics or tracking of any kind. We do not sell anything about anyone, ever.

The one thing worth knowing before handing the app to a child: a photo they pick is uploaded to our service and passed to OpenAI to be turned into a drawing. It is not stored by us. If that is not something you want, simply do not use the photo button — everything else works from typed or spoken words.

That is the complete list. The record contains nothing that identifies a person.

We also keep a record of how each drawing session went, so we can see where the app helps and where it gets in the way: the broad category asked for (animals, space, vehicles and so on, chosen from a fixed list in our code), what kind of step each request was, whether each one worked, and the date. There is no time of day and nothing written by a child — the category comes from a fixed list, so it cannot contain a name or a place.

These records are not linked to an installation. They are labelled with a random session number, so they describe a drawing session rather than a person, and there is no way for us to tell which sessions belong to the same child.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, or account of any kind. No contacts, no location, no advertising identifier. No analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers or SDKs for either. No behavioural profiling and no advertising, so nothing here is used to target anyone.

What leaves the device, and where it goes

Typed words and chosen photos. These go to our service, which passes them to OpenAI to generate the picture. We do not store either: our database has no field for them, and we keep no image storage at all. The words are used once, in passing, to pick the broad category described above — the words themselves are then gone. OpenAI processes them under its own API policy and may retain content briefly for safety and abuse monitoring before deleting it.

Spoken words, only if the microphone button is used. Speech is turned into text by Apple (on iOS) or Google (on Android) using the device's own dictation service. We never receive the audio, only the text the device produces, which is then treated exactly like typed words.

A device check. On launch the app asks Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity, via Firebase App Check, to confirm it is a genuine unmodified app. This returns a yes/no style token, not information about you. It exists so free pictures cannot be farmed by automated installs.

Nothing else leaves the device. Pictures are shared or printed only when someone chooses to, and then only to wherever they choose to send them.

What stays on the device

Finished pictures, the history of a session, and any doodles are stored only on the device. They are never uploaded except as part of making the next picture. Deleting the app deletes all of them.

Children

ColorCraft is made for children, and it is built so that a child cannot hand over personal information: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to fill in. The app never asks a child for their name, age, or any other detail about themselves.

Settings that affect money or a grown-up's decisions sit behind a simple arithmetic gate intended to be answered by an adult.

The installation record above exists to run the service and prevent abuse — authenticating the installation, tracking remaining pictures, and stopping the free allowance being farmed. It is not used to build a profile, to advertise, or for any purpose beyond operating the app.

A parent should know that a photo, if one is chosen, leaves the device. That is the only content that does. The photo button can simply be left unused.

Keeping and deleting data

The installation record is kept while the installation exists, because it holds the remaining picture count. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device; the small server-side record remains until removed on request.

To have an installation record deleted, send us the short code shown on the grown-ups screen and we will delete it. We cannot find a record without that code, because we hold nothing else that connects it to anyone.

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the current version always lives at this address.
