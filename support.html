Sketchino — Support

Sketchino (called ColorCraft Young Artist until October 2026) turns what a child says, types or photographs into a black-and-white colouring page. This page is how to reach us and what we can and cannot do.

Contact

Email colorcraft.young.artist@gmail.com. We are a very small team and answer within a few days.

Common questions

A picture did not come out right. Say or type what to change — "give the dragon a hat", "make it snowy" — and the app draws it again. The earlier picture stays in the history, so nothing is lost.

The app says the crayons are resting. All of the day's free pictures have been made. They come back tomorrow, or a grown-up can add more from the Grown-ups screen.

Where are my remaining pictures after a new phone or tablet? They live on our server against your installation's code, and that code is included in your device's own backup. Restore or migrate the device and the count comes with it. There is no "restore purchases" button because there is nothing to restore from the store: the pictures are already yours on our side.

The photo button asks a sum. A photo is the one thing that leaves the device to be turned into a drawing, so choosing one is a grown-up's decision. The same sum guards buying and the Grown-ups settings.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores on the device is removed by deleting the app, or by Erase everything on the Grown-ups screen.

To have your installation's record on our server deleted, email us the short code shown on the Grown-ups screen (it looks like AB12-CD34). That code is the only thing that connects a record to you — we hold no name, email or account — so we cannot find it without it. Deleting the record also deletes any remaining pictures on it.
