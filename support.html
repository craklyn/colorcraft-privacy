Sketchino — Support

Sketchino (called ColorCraft Young Artist until October 2026) turns what a child says, types or photographs into a black-and-white colouring page. This page is how to reach us and what we can and cannot do.

Contact

Email colorcraft.young.artist@gmail.com. We are a very small team and answer within a few days.

Common questions

A picture did not come out right. Tap the purple wand under it, then say or type what to change — "give the dragon a hat", "make it snowy" — and the app draws it again. The earlier picture stays in My pages, so nothing is lost.

The app says the crayons are resting. All of the day's free pictures have been made. They come back tomorrow, or a grown-up can add more from the Grown-ups screen (the lock at the top of the home screen).

Where are my remaining pictures after a new phone or tablet? They live on our server against your installation's code, and that code is included in your device's own backup. Restore or migrate the device and the count comes with it. There is no "restore purchases" button because there is nothing to restore from the store: the pictures are already yours on our side.

The camera door says "Photos are off". Photos start switched off, because a photo is the one thing that leaves the device to be turned into a drawing. A grown-up can switch them on once: tap the lock at the top of the home screen, answer the sum, and turn on Turn photos into pages. After that the child can use photos without asking again; switching them off asks nothing.

Where are the settings and the privacy policy? Behind the lock at the top of the home screen. Answer the sum to reach the Grown-ups screen: pictures left and buying more, picture detail and style, photos, the support code, About & privacy, and Erase all pages.

How do I delete one picture? Open My pages, press and hold the page, answer the sum, and choose Delete page. Pages that were made by changing it are kept.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores on the device is removed by deleting the app, or by Erase all pages on the Grown-ups screen (pictures left are kept).

To have your installation's record on our server deleted, email us the support code shown on the Grown-ups screen (it looks like AB12-CD34). That code is the only thing that connects a record to you — we hold no name, email or account — so we cannot find it without it. Deleting the record also deletes any remaining pictures on it.
